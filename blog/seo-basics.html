SEO Basics in Simple Words

A simple explanation of SEO, written in clear English so that anyone who builds websites can understand it.

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) is the process of making your website easier to find and understand for search engines like Google. The goal is simple: when people search for something related to your business, your pages should appear higher in the results.

Three things to remember

  1. Good content: helpful, clear and focused on the user.
  2. Clear structure: clean headings, internal links and readable layout.
  3. Technical health: fast loading, mobile-friendly, no major errors.
